Privacy Policy – Heliocore Idle

Last updated: 27/11-2026

This policy explains how Heliocore Idle ("the game"), made by Collaxter ("we", "us"), handles information. Contact: Bewulgaming@gmail.com.

1. What the game stores on your device

Your game progress (energy, generators, upgrades, Stardust, Dark Matter, relics, trophies and settings) is saved on your device. We do not run accounts or game servers, and we do not receive your save data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes the copy on your device. You can also copy a backup code from More > Save.

2. Google Play Games (cloud save, achievements and leaderboards)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the game uses Google Play Games Services to:

  • save a copy of your progress to the cloud (Saved Games), so you can restore it on another device;
  • unlock achievements;
  • submit your scores to leaderboards (energy forged, Big Bangs and comets caught).

Google stores this data with your Play Games profile. Your Play Games name and avatar can be shown to other players on leaderboards, depending on your Play Games privacy settings. We do not receive your email address or contacts. Signing in is optional, and the game works fully without it.

You can manage or delete your Play Games data, including this game's cloud save, in the Google Play Games app or settings. Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads, which only play when you choose to watch one. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the game under More > Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

4. Notifications

If you turn on notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when probes are back or the daily gift is ready). These are created on your device and are not sent through our servers.

5. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are available (for example an ad-free pass), payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought so it can be delivered in the game.

6. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Information is only processed by the service providers named above (Google Play Games, Google AdMob and Google Play) to provide their services.

8. Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled by you directly (deleting the game, deleting your Play Games data, resetting your advertising ID, changing ad consent). For any privacy question or request, contact Bewulgaming@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be at this page, with the date at the top.
