Privacy Policy – Pip: Idle Mining Cat

Last updated: 22/11-2026

This policy explains how Pip: Idle Mining Cat ("the game"), made by Collaxter ("we", "us"), handles information. Contact: Bewulgaming@gmail.com.

1. What the game stores

Your game progress (stages, gold, diamonds, upgrades, skins and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run accounts or game servers, and we do not receive your save data. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show optional rewarded video ads (for example to get Auto Upgrade time or a 4x Diamond Mine reward). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice any time in the game under Settings > Privacy & ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google's use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. In-app purchases

If in-app purchases are available, payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of which item was bought so it can be delivered in the game.

4. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. Information is only processed by the service providers named above (Google AdMob and Google Play) to provide their services.

6. Your rights

Because we don't hold personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled on your device (deleting the game, resetting your advertising ID, changing ad consent). For any privacy question or request, contact Bewulgaming@gmail.com.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be at this page, with the date at the top.
