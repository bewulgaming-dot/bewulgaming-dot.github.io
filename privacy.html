Privacy Policy for Oath Against the Horde

Last updated November 25, 2026

Collaxter develops and publishes Oath Against the Horde (the “Game”). This policy covers the Android, desktop, and browser editions. The Google Play, advertising, and reminder features described below apply to Android where available. For privacy questions and requests, contact bewulgaming@gmail.com.

Game saves and settings

The Game stores your progression and preferences on your device or in your browser. This includes unlocks, equipment, currencies, mission records, and settings. Core gameplay works offline and requires no Game account. Collaxter operates no gameplay or save-sync server and does not automatically receive your saves.

Where available, you can export a save to a file and import a file you choose. Exports contain progression and settings and may include timestamps and version information. The destination you choose, such as a cloud storage provider, handles the file under its own policy. Deleting or resetting the Game does not delete separate exports.

On Android, a dedicated progress snapshot can be included in Android system backup and device transfer. Your Android backup provider and device settings govern availability and restoration. Android 12 and later require supported encryption for cloud backup of the snapshot. The backup excludes WebView data, advertising and consent preferences, the local age setting, notification preferences, and separate purchase records. Collaxter does not receive system backups. Restore timing varies and the restored progress may be older than your last session. Active runs are not restored.

Google Play Games cloud saves

Some Android builds include Google Play Games Services. When available, Play Games may attempt to sign you in with the profile selected on your device. The Game uses the Play Games player ID to check that a backup or restore is performed for the same profile that was shown during save comparison. The Game displays your Play Games profile name in the comparison. You can play offline without using Cloud Save.

When signed in to Play Games, the Game can automatically send a progress snapshot after a run or another saved progress change, such as collecting a reward or upgrading equipment. Starting with version 1.0.42, automatic cloud backups are off until you enable them in Settings → Saves. You can turn them off there to stop future uploads. Existing cloud backups are retained. Earlier cloud-save builds could back up automatically by default. You can turn off automatic backups in Saves in those builds. The same screen offers a separate manual backup with confirmation. Checking for a backup does not create one or upload progress. The snapshot can include unlocked content, achievements recorded in your save, currencies, active missions, game settings, the game version, and a backup timestamp. The upload excludes your local age category, ad consent and notification settings, purchase tokens, and the private Gem delivery record. Google stores the snapshot for your Play Games profile and encrypts the data in transit. Collaxter does not run a separate save server or receive a copy of the snapshot.

A cloud comparison may ask you to review an existing backup before automatic uploads continue. You choose whether to restore it or manually back up this device. Before a restore, the Game keeps a local recovery copy that you can export from Settings → Saves. To remove a cloud backup, use Google's Play Games data controls for Oath Against the Horde. Resetting progress on your device leaves your cloud backup in place. Play Games data controls

Google may process your Play Games gamer identity, SDK analytics, and diagnostics. See Google's privacy policy and Play Games disclosure for details. Google Privacy Policy · Play Games data disclosure

Age settings, rewarded ads, and privacy choices

The Android Game can ask for your birth date to select an advertising age category. The date is used on your device to calculate the category and is then discarded. You may decline to provide it. The Game stores the broad category locally, and sends the applicable privacy treatment to Google rather than the date you enter. You can change the category in Options under Privacy. The Game applies general-audience and restricted advertising settings for players under 18 and players who decline to provide age information.

Android uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform for optional rewarded ads and advertising privacy choices. The Game requests non-personalized ads when Google's privacy SDK permits requests. Ads can offer a revive, daily supplies, or an increased Gold or Oathfire Gem mission reward. The Game works without watching ads. Where Google makes it available, use Options → Privacy choices to review or change your advertising choices. Android also provides device-level advertising controls where supported.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK can process information such as your IP address, device and account identifiers including the Android advertising ID where available, interactions with ads or the app, and diagnostics. Google uses this information for purposes including delivering and measuring ads, analytics, and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still involve this processing. Google's privacy notices explain its handling of information and available controls. Google Privacy Policy · Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure

Google Play purchases and updates

On Android, Google Play processes payment for the optional Ad-Free Upgrade and repeatable Oathfire Gem packs. The Game receives transaction information from Google Play, including product identifiers, purchase tokens, purchase state and quantity, and signature information, to verify, deliver, and restore eligible purchases. Collaxter does not receive your card or bank details through the Game. The Game verifies purchases on the device and uses a private local record containing a hash of the purchase token to prevent duplicate Gem delivery. That record is excluded from save exports and Android system backups.

Verified Ad-Free ownership is cached locally for offline use and refreshed through Google Play when available. Use Restore purchases with the Google Play account used for the purchase. A save import does not restore Ad-Free ownership. Once a Gem pack has been delivered, its Gems are part of local progression. Recovering that balance after reinstalling or switching devices depends on restoring a save that contains it. Google Play Terms · Google Privacy Policy

The Android edition uses Google Play In-App Updates to check for updates and offer installation through Google Play. Google documents that its service uses device metadata, the installed app version, and information about installed modules or asset packs to determine update availability and size. The Game stores its update reminder preference locally. Google Play In-App Updates data disclosure

Optional Android reminders

Android can remind you when scouts return from a mission and, if separately enabled, when daily supplies become available after the next 09:00 local reset following a claim. The Game schedules reminders on your device using your saved progression, reminder settings, and local time. Android asks for permission where required. Settings stay on the device. The Game uses no remote notification server or push token for these reminders. Android can restore eligible schedules after reboot and recalculate the daily schedule after a time-zone change.

You can turn each reminder off in Game Options or disable notifications in Android settings. Depending on your lock-screen settings, a mission reminder may show the reward on your screen. Tapping a reminder opens the relevant Game screen. It does not claim the reward.

Support, external links, and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to share. We use them to respond, investigate problems, and meet applicable obligations. Where EU or UK data protection law applies, responding to support and investigating issues relies on our legitimate interests in operating and supporting the Game, and we retain information where required to meet legal obligations. We keep correspondence for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes. Please avoid sending payment credentials or unnecessary personal information.

These public policy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. GitHub Pages data collection

Links in the Game may open external websites or services. Those services have their own privacy policies. Google and any storage or email providers you use handle their own processing and retention under their policies.

You can remove local data by resetting progress where available, clearing app or browser data, or uninstalling. Delete any separately stored exports or system backups through the service where they are kept. Deleting local game data does not remove Google Play purchase records or information already processed by Google.

Your rights and contacting us

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information, object to certain processing, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. Contact Collaxter at bewulgaming@gmail.com about information we hold. Use Google's privacy and account controls for information Google handles under its own policy.
